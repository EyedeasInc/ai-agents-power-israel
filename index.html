ABOUT THE EVENT

AI POWER AGENTS is now in its third edition, evolving from its original roots as “Dynamics Power Israel.” The Biz Apps community in Israel is one of the most vibrant in the region, and this conference exists to give that community a home for deep technical content, real-world stories, and meaningful networking. The event is for:

Who it's for
  • AI Professionals building plugins, integrations, or custom agents
  • Power Platform makers, admins, and consultants
  • Dynamics 365 Customer Engagement implementers and ISVs
  • Cloud solution architects and tech leads
  • Business leaders driving digital transformation with Microsoft tech
CORE CONTENT
We’ll bring perspectives from Microsoft MVPs, partners, and practitioners who design and run Business Applications every day — highlighting how AI agents are changing how they plan, build, and operate solutions.

History & Highlights

From Dynamics Power Israel to AI POWER AGENTS.

The conference began as Dynamics Power Israel, a grassroots gathering for local Dynamics 365 professionals. As low-code and AI became central to how Israeli organizations build solutions, the event evolved into AI POWER AGENTS Community Conference.
