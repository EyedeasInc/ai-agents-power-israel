About the event

Where Business Applications meet AI agents.

AI POWER AGENTS is now in its third edition, evolving from its original roots as “Dynamics Power Israel.” The Biz Apps community in Israel is one of the most vibrant in the region, and this conference exists to give that community a home for deep technical content, real-world stories, and meaningful networking.

For makers & consultants

See how others are using AI to accelerate delivery: building apps faster, automating complex processes, and augmenting users with copilots instead of replacing them.

  • Model-driven & canvas apps with AI infused flows
  • Copilot Studio, plugins, virtual agents, and more
  • Practices you can reuse the moment you get back

For architects & tech leads

Learn reference architectures, governance patterns, and design decisions behind AI-ready Business Applications on the Microsoft stack.

  • Dataverse, Dynamics 365, and Power Pages at scale
  • Agentic patterns using MCPs and external services
  • Security, compliance, and responsible AI in practice

For leaders & decision makers

Hear how organizations are moving from experiments to value: rolling out AI-assisted experiences that users actually adopt.

  • Use-cases that resonate with business stakeholders
  • Change management for AI-powered workflows
  • Measuring impact and ROI across teams
History & Highlights

From Dynamics Power Israel to AI POWER AGENTS.

The conference began as Dynamics Power Israel, a grassroots gathering for local Dynamics 365 professionals. As low-code and AI became central to how Israeli organizations build solutions, the event evolved into AI POWER AGENTS Community Conference, reflecting a broader focus on Power Platform, AI agents, and copilots.

  • 2019 Edition: Dynamics Power Israel — Dynamics 365–centric, early Biz Apps community roots.
  • 2023 Edition: Expansion into Power Platform, automation, and early AI-powered scenarios.
  • 2026 Edition: AI POWER AGENTS at Microsoft Herzliya — full focus on agentic AI, copilots, and end-to-end Biz Apps solutions.

Hosting the event at the Microsoft Herzliya Campus makes this edition truly special. The campus is a sustainable “city of work” with air-to-water generators, a pet playground, advanced accessibility features, and flexible green spaces — all designed to blend high-tech innovation with employee well-being and a vibrant community feel.

Looking ahead to the 2026 edition, the vision is to host 300+ community members for a full day of amazing content, networking, and app building — all accelerated by AI.
